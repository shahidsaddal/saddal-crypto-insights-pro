Market scanner

Ranks BUY/SELL setups across Binance pairs. Uses the market selected in Analyze.
Signal strength is a 0–100 setup score: multi-timeframe alignment 28%, indicator confirmation 28%, momentum 20%, volume and liquidity 14%, market conditions 10%. It is not a probability of profit or a win rate. Scanning uses price and volume only; funding and news context are not scored, and the event-risk gate still applies. Each pair costs two data requests; if Binance returns a rate-limit error the scan stops and says so.

Backtester & trade filters

Replays this engine candle-by-candle on the selected pair/timeframe/market. Stop and target are checked on candle highs/lows (stop first if both are touched). Costs included. Past results do not predict future results.
Choose a pair, market and timeframe in Analyze, then press Run backtest. Equity, risk and cost fields are used for costs. The event-risk gate is ignored in backtests.
Two exit models are compared: Full exit at TP1, and Managed (half off at TP1, stop to break-even, rest to TP2). Paper trades now use the managed model and candle-based checks. Use the first-half / second-half rows to spot overfitting.

Trade history

P&L convention: Gross P&L = direction × (exit/current price − entry price) × quantity. Net P&L subtracts estimated opening and closing commissions based on the selected market and Maker/Taker execution types. The full estimated round-trip commission is reserved while a position is open; actual closing commission is incurred only when the position closes. Open and closed position tables show gross P&L, estimated fees and net P&L separately. Open a trade with “Paper trade this signal” in the signal panel or the Paper button in the scanner. Positions are checked against 60-second price snapshots while this page is open; levels touched between snapshots can be missed. Stop gaps use the observed price when worse than the stop. Round-trip costs are approximated. This paper simulator does not model historical funding payments, exchange maintenance margin, liquidation, partial fills, or tick-level execution; futures results are not an exchange-accurate simulation. Spot is long-only. A few paper trades prove nothing: validate on unseen historical data and forward paper trading before judging a strategy.

Entry timer & trigger confirmation

Check a planned entry against a live Binance quote or the last completed candle. This is a trigger check, not a guarantee of execution.
The candle confirmation uses the most recent completed candle, not the still-forming candle. Network or exchange rate limits can prevent a check.

Exit planner

Calculate stop-loss, multiple take-profit levels, risk/reward and projected P&L before taking a trade.
Estimates exclude funding, slippage, spread, liquidation fees and gaps. For futures, leverage changes margin and liquidation risk but does not reduce the price-distance loss on a given position size.

Position sizing & risk budget

Size a crypto position from account equity, risk percentage and stop distance.
The calculation caps notional to account equity for spot, or equity × leverage for futures, unless you enter a lower cap. It is a planning estimate and does not query exchange-specific step sizes or minimum orders.

Risk manager & trade guardrails

Configure limits for paper trades. These checks are enforced in the local simulator, not on a live exchange.
Daily realized P&L is counted from closed paper-trade records for the current local date. Open positions are not reliably marked to market for this daily-loss gate; monitor unrealized P&L separately. This is a browser-side simulator control and can be bypassed by modifying the page. It is not suitable for protecting real funds.
